Q: Why return &pb.UserProfileResponse{UserId: user.ID, Email: user.Email, Name: user.Name}
A: Instead of returning a full struct, returning a pointer improves performance

Generate gRPC client and server outputs from root of service
  From /user-service, run
  $ protoc --go_out=./proto --go-grpc_out=./proto proto/user_service.proto
